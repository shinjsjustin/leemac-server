# Commentary, Leemac documents v1

What I went for: a set that reads as one hand. Every letter page has the same masthead, the same three bands (who, the one number that matters, the facts) and the same table; only the facts and the close change. The label is the same idea scaled for a thermal printer: black only, the ship-to as the loudest thing on it.

What I decided along the way:
- Facts are label-over-value cells that drop out when empty, so "no Attn" or "no PO yet" leaves nothing behind (the 25-part invoice proves it).
- No fills anywhere: the old cyan and pink bands cost toner and grey out on the mono Brother. Rules and type weight carry the hierarchy; the navy accent prints as near-black.
- The invoice's "Due date" is the delivery date in the DB; I labelled it "Delivery due" and added a computed "Payment due" so AP can't confuse the two.
- First pass repeated Leadtime and Prepared by in three places each, and the totals table stretched to the notes' height; both fixed after looking at the PNGs.

What it says about me: I trust the table to do the work and spend the boldness on two things per page: the customer and the money.
