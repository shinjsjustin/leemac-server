Leemac documents, v1

Five documents as one family: Leemac's header and logo on every letter page, the customer and the one number that matters at top right (total, amount due, pieces shipped), a row of labelled facts, then the parts. Black plus one deep navy that prints as near-black on the Brother; the label is black only. Dates are written "Oct 30, 2026". Sample data is invented. Click a page to open its PDF.

Fields I added beyond your list (please confirm each)

  1. Fax and email in the header. Fax (408) 573-0131 from the old sheet; email leemac@leemac.co from the site's About page and the mailer.
  2. Details line under each description (*material *finish, or the part's details). The old forms had it as a second row; it tells the receiver which part is which.
  3. Line number (#) column. The old Item #.
  4. Invoice: "Attn Accounts Payable" on the Bill to, and the client's Attn on the Ship to. As on the old invoice.
  5. Invoice date and Payment due (invoice date + 30 days). Payment due is new and computed; your "Due date" is shown on the invoice as "Delivery due" so AP doesn't read it as the payment date.
  6. Ship via: Leemac delivery on Order, Packing List and Invoice. The old forms said LEEMAC.
  7. Remit-to details: "Checks payable to Leemac Manufacturing Inc." and "Please quote invoice # with payment". No bank details were added.
  8. Lines and pieces count on the Packing List, the label and the Quote headline. Lets the receiver count boxes against the paper.
  9. Page footer with the document's numbers and "Page X of Y" on every letter page.
